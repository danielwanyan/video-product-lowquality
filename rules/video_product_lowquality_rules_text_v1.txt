Video Product Lowquality Rules v4

Project scope
Review one e-commerce short video, one bound product evidence package, and user feedback evidence. Output one video-level decision and separate evidence details for video content risk, product or merchant risk, and comment or review risk.

This project reviews all e-commerce videos uniformly. Do not create separate labels for AI-generated versus non-AI-generated videos. Synthetic, unrealistic, or impossible visuals are evidence types, not project labels.

Evidence channels
Video content evidence: video frames from frame_list, ASR, OCR, and video URL.
Product and merchant evidence: product_id, seller_id_str, country, and product reference images from images.
Comment and review evidence: comments, CCR tags, comment text, and product_review_summary.

Final decisions
problematic: strong evidence shows a real video, product, merchant, or feedback risk.
manual_review: evidence is concerning but incomplete, visual evidence is missing while comments or reviews are risky, or fake/IPR/mismatch risk is plausible but not proven.
clean: available video, product, merchant, comment, and review evidence do not show meaningful risk.

Rule V1: video_product_mismatch
Hit when video frames show a physical product being promoted or demonstrated, and the shown product does not match the bound product reference images.
Compare against all product reference images, not only the first image.
If the shown product matches any product reference image in same style/type and there is no material contradiction in size, dimensions, capacity, count, or scale, do not hit mismatch for that product.
Use product image visual features such as type, shape, structure, material appearance, packaging, size labels, dimensions, visible scale, and core design.
Same product style or same product type is not enough to clear mismatch when the video materially changes the promised size, dimensions, capacity, count, or scale.
Hit video_product_mismatch when product reference images or visible product-page text say one dimension or size variant, but video frames make the promoted item appear to be a materially larger or smaller variant.
Example: product reference image says the cat house is 40 x 40 x 34 cm / 34 cm high, while the video frame presents the cat house as a much taller large cylinder relative to the adult presenter or pet; this is size/scale货不对板 even if the color, shape, opening, scratching panel, and hanging ball match.
When this kind of visible size/scale contradiction is clear, output problematic with primary_issue_type=video_product_mismatch.
Do not use metadata alone to prove visual consistency.
Ignore lighting, minor color shade, angle, render-versus-camera style, packaging language, brand OCR text, decorative props, and ordinary apparel wearing-style differences.
If no physical product appears in video frames, do not hit video_product_mismatch from ASR/OCR alone.

Rule V2: video_exaggerated_promotion
Hit when video frames, ASR, or OCR present product function, effect, size, quality, capacity, speed, result, or use scenario in a way that materially overstates what the product can reasonably do.
Examples include impossible before/after effects, unrealistic instant results, exaggerated capacity, toy or small product producing full-size effects, small device shown with professional-level power, and visual abundance far beyond the sold unit.
When the video demonstrates unusually strong capability, fit, sealing, durability, stretch, or broad compatibility, and product_review_summary or clustered comments say the real product is too small, too thin, tears easily, does not fit, or is misleading compared to promotional videos, the primary issue should be primary_issue_type=video_exaggerated_promotion. Keep poor_product_quality and product_side_mismatch_or_exaggeration as secondary product-side hits when supported.
Example: a stretch food cover video shows strong sealing and broad fit over watermelon, trays, bowls, and containers, while reviews say the covers are too small/thin, tear easily, and do not match promotional videos. This is content-side exaggerated promotion as the main issue, plus product quality / product-side mismatch as supporting product issues.
Reserve strong hits for concrete evidence. Weak marketing praise alone is not enough.

Rule V3: video_unrealistic_or_continuity_error
Hit when video frames show physical impossibility, continuity error, product identity drift, structure drift, pattern drift, content identity drift, impossible scale or pose change, impossible emitted smoke/mist/fire/light, or a scene that would mislead consumers about the real product.
For process videos, compare input and output within the same segment. Do not compare unrelated montage segments.
For foldable, telescopic, adjustable, or assembled household products, check whether hinges, support bars, crossbars, feet, panels, and side frames can physically move between adjacent frames as shown. If the frame expands, collapses, or changes geometry in a way the reference product cannot support, the main issue should be primary_issue_type=video_unrealistic_or_continuity_error.
Check hand interaction and object contact. If a hand appears to hold multiple objects with an impossible grip, floats through the product, changes contact points inconsistently, or the product responds without plausible force/support, treat it as a continuity or physical-reality error when it affects product understanding.
For transparent storage boxes, shoe cabinets, organizers, drawers, or containers, check object materialization and drawer contents across adjacent frames. If a box appears suddenly, shoes appear inside a drawer without a visible placement action, drawer contents change between 1-second frames, or a temporary white box flashes and is then replaced by shoes, the main issue should be primary_issue_type=video_unrealistic_or_continuity_error.
Do not dismiss object materialization as normal editing when the frames are consecutive product-use steps and the change affects the claimed storage or organization function.
Example: a foldable microwave shelf reference image shows a 45 cm high frame with fixed side supports, hooks, panels, and telescopic width, but video frames show the side frame and support bars stretching or changing geometry unrealistically around a large oven, or a hand carrying several spice jars with an impossible grip. This is content-side 穿帮/不符现实 even if the product style matches the reference image.
Example: a transparent shoe storage video shows an empty or partly empty compartment, then one second later a white box flashes into the compartment, and the next frame shoes appear inside without a plausible hand placement. This is object materialization / drawer contents continuity error.
For food and packaged goods, prioritize sold SKU contradictions such as form, wrapper, count, or visible quantity mismatch.
Do not over-focus on noisy OCR, minor hand artifacts, or ordinary cooking/color changes when the product remains consistent.

Rule V4: race_conflict_or_fake_police
Hit when the video uses racial conflict, ethnic hostility, fake police, fake law-enforcement authority, staged enforcement, or other high-conflict social/political identity framing to promote or dramatize the product.
General uniforms, public service imagery, or ordinary safety scenes are not enough unless the content implies false authority, intimidation, conflict, or discriminatory framing.

Rule V5: staged_mass_production_or_pure_marketing
Hit when ASR/OCR is mainly sales pressure, price, urgency, discount, buying guidance, or repetitive marketing, while providing no substantive product information.
Substantive product information includes function, material, size, capacity, ingredients, usage, compatibility, use scenario, operation method, or realistic performance explanation.
Do not hit this rule when the video has weak but real product information. Weak detail may support manual_review or other rules, but is not automatically pure marketing.

Rule P1: ipr_or_counterfeit
Hit or manual_review when product images, video frames, or feedback suggest fake, counterfeit, unauthorized brand/IP use, copied character/team/logo, or brand impersonation.
Use visual brand/IP signals plus comment/review evidence. Do not rely on title or category alone.
Strong fake/counterfeit evidence can be problematic. Weak or uncertain suspicion should be manual_review.

Rule P2: poor_product_quality
Hit when comment/review evidence or visuals show poor durability, poor material, broken item, dirty/leaking product, safety concern, bad workmanship, or repeated concrete quality complaints.
Clustered comment/review evidence is high-weight because many product quality problems are not visible from product images or video frames.
Single vague dissatisfaction is not enough by itself.

Rule P3: product_side_mismatch_or_exaggeration
Hit or manual_review when comments, reviews, product images, or video evidence indicate received product does not match the video/product promise, the effect does not work, size/material differs, or promotion is materially misleading.
Use comment/review clusters such as not as described, wrong item, poor effect, perceived scam, refund/return problem, or mismatch complaints.

Comment and review weighting
CCR tags are structured evidence. Use them with comment text and product_review_summary.
ProductIssue--ProductQuality maps to poor_product_quality.
ProductIssue--ProductUserPerceptionProblem maps to not_as_described, sizing, or perception risk.
MisleadingPromotionIssue maps to product_side_mismatch_or_exaggeration.
ContentIssue or Monitoring tags about ArtificialIntelligenceGeneratedContent map to ai_or_unrealistic_video signal.
ProductIssue--PriceComplain maps to price_or_value_complaint and should not by itself become poor quality.
PlatformIssue maps to platform issue only and should not by itself become product risk.

Data availability
If frame_list is empty, do not make video-frame visual claims.
If images is empty or invalid, do not make product-image comparison claims.
If ASR and OCR are empty, do not hit pure marketing from missing text.
If comments are empty or only placeholders, do not claim clustered user feedback.
If key visual evidence is missing but comments/reviews are high risk, prefer manual_review.

Output requirements
Return raw JSON only in the project schema.
Use Chinese summary and reasons for human review.
Every hit or uncertain result must include concrete evidence.
Explain whether comment/review evidence changed the final decision.
