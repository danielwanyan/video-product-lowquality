[video_product_mismatch] 挂车商品与讲解商品不一致
Triggers:
- Video frames or ASR/OCR clearly promote or explain a main product that conflicts with product images in core category, function, form, visible quantity, key specification, size, dimension, or promised use. Same product style does not clear a material size or dimension contradiction.
- Product images visibly promise a product type, quantity, size, form, or function, and the video promotes a materially different product.
False-positive boundaries:
- Do not hit for color-only differences.
- Do not hit for ordinary model variation when category, core function, core structure, quantity, visible dimension, and promised use remain compatible.
- Do not hit for minor style, material, texture, lighting, angle, packaging language, ordinary brand-text differences, or functional words printed on a product such as MUSIC SOUND.
- Do not use brand text, OCR text, product-page wording, or package language alone as IPP evidence unless the video is clearly promoting a different physical product.
- Do not hit merely because the video shows more units than one product image when the sold set can include those units.
- Do not hit from opening scenes, props, outfits, or background objects unless that item is promoted as the sold product.
Required evidence:
- video frame or ASR/OCR evidence of the promoted item
- product image evidence for the bound product
- specific core contradiction

[misleading_functionality_or_effect] 功能效果虚假夸大
Triggers:
- Video frames, ASR, or OCR make a concrete misleading claim about efficacy, speed, durability, medical or health effect, quantity, time, before/after result, safety, or physical capability.
- The claim must be supported by frame_list, ASR, or OCR from the video itself; product images are auxiliary only.
- The video demonstration visually implies a product effect that is physically impossible or materially misleading to consumers.
False-positive boundaries:
- Do not hit from product images alone.
- Do not use product detail page claims or product image marketing copy by themselves to decide that the video is exaggerated. 商品详情页或商品图文案不能单独触发视频夸大。
- Do not hit for ordinary advertising tone, subjective praise, metaphor, fashion or fit claims without concrete false promise, or expected cleaning, shaving, styling, cooking, or demonstration outcomes.
- Do not hit when the claim is a common category claim and the video itself does not make a stronger promise than the visible product evidence.
- Minor numerical ambiguity that can reasonably be ordinary speech error is not enough.
- A concrete claim is not automatically misleading. It must be materially stronger than the bound product or contradicted by concrete product evidence, or the demonstrated effect must be physically impossible.
- Do not hit for vague metaphors, ordinary category effects, routine marketing language, or one-off ordinary speech error when there is no material consumer deception.
- If the video claim is consistent with the bound product and is not materially more exaggerated, choose clean even when the wording is strong.
- Ordinary sales language, plausible category benefits, and user/device-dependent outcomes are clean without a direct hard contradiction, absolute guaranteed outcome, or physically impossible claim.
- A 5000mAh power-bank claim about charging a phone twice is clean when no exact phone battery, charging conditions, or device-specific hard contradiction is established.
- Common pillow comfort, posture, facial-appearance, and neck-tension benefit language is a plausible category benefit unless it becomes an absolute cure, permanent anatomical-change claim, or universal guarantee.
Required evidence:
- specific frame, ASR, or OCR claim
- explanation of why the claim is materially misleading
- at least one decisive basis: direct hard contradiction, absolute guaranteed outcome, or physically impossible claim

[suspected_pirated_or_reused_content] 疑似盗版 / 疑似盗剪
Triggers:
- The video strongly suggests reused or pirated content, such as Chinese domestic studio recording cues, Chinese UI or environment not fitting the target market, blurry reposted footage, watermark-hiding layout, or obvious unrelated creator/content reuse.
- The video has a clear piracy or reposting pattern that affects authenticity of the e-commerce promotion.
- A strong multi-source presenter inconsistency can qualify without a watermark when multiple clearly different presenters appear with inconsistent clothing, locations, capture quality, or filming style and no coherent same-creator or demonstration explanation exists.
False-positive boundaries:
- Do not hit for replying to a user comment.
- Do not hit for showing another creator briefly and then self-verifying the product.
- Do not hit for proof-style self-comparison, product validation, or debunking content.
- Do not hit only because the same creator appears in different scenes.
- Do not hit for generic blurry footage, simple edits, style shifts, ordinary b-roll, or a scene transition without a concrete copied/reused-content signal.
- A transition from introductory scenery or lifestyle b-roll into a substantive product demonstration is not piracy or reuse by itself.
- A Tier B suspected-reuse hit requires a concrete source-authenticity signal beyond scene, environment, style, or packaging-language change.
- Ordinary b-roll, one creator changing scenes, coherent comparisons, reply videos, and coherent product demonstrations are clean and block the strong multi-source presenter path.
- is_aigc is not piracy evidence. Never use is_aigc=1 to support suspected piracy or reuse.
Required evidence:
- specific visual or textual piracy/reuse signal
- for a regular Tier B hit, a concrete source-authenticity signal rather than ordinary b-roll or scene transition alone
- for the no-watermark exception, concrete cross-frame evidence of a strong multi-source presenter inconsistency

[pure_marketing_pitch] 仅营销叫卖
Triggers:
- Nearly all ASR/OCR is price, urgency, stock, discount, order guidance, or sales pressure.
- The video contains almost no product function, material, size, use scenario, operation method, or realistic product information.
False-positive boundaries:
- Weak but real product information should usually prevent this hit.
- Do not hit when the video provides substantive product features, material, size, usage, softness/comfort, compatibility, operation, or scenario information, even if it also contains buying guidance.
- Do not hit from price, discount, urgency, or purchase CTA alone when visual demonstration provides meaningful product information.
- Visible wearing, trying on, operating, applying, assembling, or other real product use is substantive product content and blocks this label unless the use is only a token moment and marketing pressure still dominates nearly the whole video.
Required evidence:
- ASR or OCR evidence showing near-total marketing pitch

[no_physical_product_display] 无实物展示（围绕商品讲解）
Triggers:
- The video talks about or promotes the product but does not show the physical product or a substantive product-use scene.
False-positive boundaries:
- AI-generated style does not equal no physical product display.
- Do not hit if the sampled frames clearly show the bound product or a substantive use scene.
- Do not hit only because product display is brief when it is clear and relevant.
- A visible package, an identifiable product being worn, or a real application, operation, installation, or use action counts as physical product display.
- A phone screen, phone gallery, overlaid picture, sticker, or separate bound-product image does not count as physical product display in the video.
- If unrelated content dominates the video, classify the issue as irrelevant_product_promotion instead of also adding no_physical_product_display. Reserve no_physical_product_display for product-focused speech or promotion whose product is not shown.
Required evidence:
- video frame evidence that product is not shown

[irrelevant_product_promotion] 内容与商品无关推广
Triggers:
- Most of the video body is unrelated to the bound product, and the product appears only briefly, at the end, or not substantively.
- The video uses unrelated entertainment, celebrity, movie-like, animal, landscape, political, sports, or other non-product content as the main body.
False-positive boundaries:
- Do not hit merely because the opening has setup or a short unrelated segment.
- Do not hit if a later segment provides real product demonstration or explanation.
- Do not hit when the product has substantive screen presence, including approximately one third of the sampled video, unless the remaining unrelated content clearly dominates the promotional message.
Required evidence:
- video frame or ASR/OCR evidence of unrelated main content

[unrealistic_or_continuity_error] 穿帮 / 不符现实
Triggers:
- When is_aigc=1, increase attention for impossible physics, hand-object contact errors, product geometry drift, materialization/disappearance, and identity drift; concrete visual evidence is still required.
- Strong visual evidence shows physically impossible product behavior, object materialization or disappearance, impossible structure deformation, impossible body or hand contact, or an impossible product effect.
- Strong examples include directly taking an item from a sealed or unopened package without opening it, a connector, plug, hook, screw, support bar, or mounting structure changing impossibly, or a hand/object contact that cannot physically perform the shown action.
- The issue affects consumer understanding of the product.
- The change cannot reasonably be explained by the sampling interval, normal editing, camera angle, or missing intermediate action.
- A finger or hand appears to press, insert, fasten, or manipulate a screw, pin, connector, or mounting part in a physically impossible way, without the required tool or feasible contact.
False-positive boundaries:
- is_aigc=1 alone must never create a hit; it is an attention multiplier only.
- is_aigc=1 only enables review and never creates a hit by itself.
- Do not hit for ordinary action jumps between sampled frames.
- Do not hit for missing intermediate actions alone.
- Do not hit for slight hand artifacts, lighting or angle changes, motion blur, or process-video cuts that do not affect product understanding.
- If uncertain, choose clean.
- is_aigc=0 does not suppress a continuity issue when concrete visual evidence independently supports it.
Required evidence:
- specific sampled frame evidence
- reason the issue cannot be explained by sparse sampling or normal editing

[sexual_or_vulgar_hook] 擦边低俗
Triggers:
- The video uses explicit sexual or adult language or a clear sexualized, dating, or sexual-reward hook for promotion, including phrases such as spend the night with me.
False-positive boundaries:
- Do not hit ordinary try-on, gym, swimwear, shapewear, or body-fit display without clear vulgar/adult framing.
- Do not hit a contextually reasonable isolated profanity that is not the promotional hook.
- Do not hit ordinary intimate grooming or pubic-hair shaving without sexualized promotional framing.
Required evidence:
- video frame, ASR, or OCR evidence of vulgar or adult framing

[disgusting_or_terrifying_visual] 恶心恐怖
Triggers:
- A strongly disturbing visual subject fills, dominates, or persists across the presentation, such as severe infestation, injury, dense-phobia-like imagery, bodily or horror-style content.
- Large-volume viscous, slimy, glue-like, mucus-like, or otherwise strongly unappetizing material is lifted, stretched, handled, or ingested in a way that dominates the visual presentation and creates clear disgust, even when it is technically an edible product.
False-positive boundaries:
- Do not hit for mildly unattractive, brief, localized, or non-dominant visuals.
- Do not hit when dirt, a dirty toilet, or similar material appears as the before, during, or after state in a cleaning product demonstration.
- Do not hit humor, a calendar, packaging, printed imagery, novelty content, or product art merely because it depicts an unpleasant subject.
- Do not hit for normal product demonstration.
Required evidence:
- specific visual evidence describing strength, screen coverage, and persistence
- evidence that the disturbing subject dominates and is not cleaning-demo or novelty/product-art context

[pirated_content] 盗版内容
Triggers:
- The video uses clearly pirated copyrighted video content as the main promotional body.
False-positive boundaries:
- Do not hit merely for reference clips, reply/comment context, or same-creator multi-scene editing when product demonstration remains original.
Required evidence:
- clear pirated content evidence

[description_not_detailed] 内容介绍不详细（低质）
Triggers:
- The video fails to provide meaningful product information for categories where function or usage explanation is required, such as supplements, health products, electronics, tools, or appliances.
False-positive boundaries:
- Do not hit for apparel, simple goods, or visual products when the product is clearly shown.
- Do not hit when visual demonstration itself provides enough product information.
- For simple apparel, accessories, plush toys, lamps, decor, and similar visual goods, clear product display is usually sufficient unless the category needs function or safety explanation.
- A clearly identifiable product name and package that communicate the core product purpose count as meaningful product information, including for supplements or health products. Do not require ingredients, mechanism, dosage, or detailed efficacy when the name and package already make the basic product and purpose clear.
Required evidence:
- category or video context showing explanation is required
- absence of meaningful explanation in frames, ASR, and OCR

[out_of_app_transaction] 站外引流交易
Triggers:
- The video clearly instructs users to transact or contact outside the platform using an external website, QR code, off-platform channel, or external transaction instruction.
False-positive boundaries:
- Do not hit TikTok in-app purchase guidance.
- Do not hit private-message requests by themselves.
Required evidence:
- ASR, OCR, or frame evidence of external transaction path

[still_frame] 静止帧
Triggers:
- All sampled frames are effectively identical in content, composition, angle, color, lighting, pose, hand position, product position, and product state.
- The all-frame comparison finds no meaningful visual change and sparse sampling still provides concrete evidence that the video remains immobile.
False-positive boundaries:
- Do not hit when frames show any meaningful visual change, including content shift, composition shift, camera angle shift, color shift, lighting shift, pose shift, hand position, product position, or product-state change.
- Do not hit merely because ASR/OCR is weak or absent when frames show clear product use or changing visual evidence.
- Do not hit a slideshow, PPT-style presentation, or sequence of different still images; switching between different images is visual change.
- If sparse sampling cannot prove that the full video remains visually identical, choose clean.
Required evidence:
- concrete all-sampled-frame comparison evidence proving effective identity and no meaningful visual change
- a non-empty frame change summary covering all sampled video frames and excluding bound-product images
